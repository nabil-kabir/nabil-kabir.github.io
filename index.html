Case files

Selected outcomes.

Company data stays confidential, so these are the stories behind the numbers. Case 05 is a live project you can open.

  • Problem
    Leadership waited half a working day for priority numbers.
    Approach
    Ranked every reporting cycle by decision impact, then automated the top tier end to end.
    Result
    Management wait time fell from 4 hours to 20 minutes, so decisions now start the same morning.
  • Problem
    High-aging stock was drifting toward expiry, and write-off, across the region.
    Approach
    Built aging-risk analysis and orchestrated regional stakeholder responses to liquidate in time.
    Result
    $100,000 in inventory value protected before it could turn into loss.
  • Problem
    The Daraz Bangladesh retail channel was running at a negative gross margin.
    Approach
    Targeted commercial strategy across assortment, promotions, pricing and bundling, steered by data.
    Result
    A 7-point swing, from −5% to +2% gross margin.
  • Problem
    Global supply-chain leadership had data everywhere, but no single weekly narrative.
    Approach
    Launched and ran the WBR program with a five-analyst team spread across three countries.
    Result
    One trusted, weekly view of the business for management review.
  • Problem
    Which stock will pass 180 days before it sells, and what is the cheapest way out? Shown on 104,884 real marketplace orders.
    Approach
    Simulated first in, first out inventory from real demand in Python, then a Power BI model with a what if liquidation planner.
    Result
    R$494K of stock found at risk. Marking it down 30% now, instead of clearing it later, saves about R$197K.
